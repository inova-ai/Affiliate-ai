V10 Visual AI QC

New API:
POST /api/qc-visual {file, referenceImage?, product?, character?, prompt?, fps?, maxFrames?}
POST /api/qc-visual-retry {qc, attempt?, maxRetries?}

Set VISION_QC_URL and optional VISION_QC_API_KEY to activate semantic vision inspection.
Without it, the result is technical-only/manual-review rather than a fake semantic pass.
